01 / The insight

A shock is only
the beginning.

A portfolio’s risk depends on what others are forced to do. We make that hypothesis explicit: model the constraints, trace the responses, and test the resulting path.

01 / EVENT→

A market shock

A repricing changes returns, volatility and available liquidity.

02 / CONSTRAINT→

A limit is crossed

Risk budgets, trend signals or drawdown limits change desired exposure.

03 / REACTION→

Actions interact

Position changes meet market depth, changing the state for the next decision.

04 / CONSEQUENCE↗

Your risk changes

Compare the portfolio path and inspect which assumptions drive the difference.

The Bank of England’s 2024 system-wide exercise found that firms’ collective actions can amplify an initial shock. This supports the problem, not MicroWorld’s model. Read the source ↗

03 / The first product

A decision tool.
A research discipline.

Our initial buyer hypothesis is an APAC investment team reviewing concentrated US equity and ETF exposure. The first job: prepare a defensible event-risk discussion.

INPUT / 01

Bring a portfolio
and a question.

Authorized holdings, a dated market snapshot and an explicit scenario. Begin with a bounded universe and a repeatable committee workflow.

First commercial step → scoped design-partner pilot

WORKSPACE / 02

Inspect the path,
not just the number.

Compare with simple baselines. Trace rule triggers. Challenge parameters. Preserve the assumptions behind the discussion.

Revenue hypothesis → annual team subscription

DISTRIBUTION / 03

Prove repeatability.
Then embed it.

Extend a validated workflow into an API for investment platforms, with usage limits and measured delivery costs.

Expansion hypothesis → platform license + usage

04 / What capital unlocks

Fund the next proof.

Each stage has a question to answer and a condition to advance. Capital buys the data, talent and time to test the thesis rigorously.

Angel / First 90 days01

Does the mechanism
add information?

Freeze the evaluation protocol, secure data rights, calibrate a bounded engine and compare held-out results with strong baselines and ablations.

Evidence gateReproducible evaluation, uncertainty intervals and an independent technical review. Narrow the claim if there is no reliable gain.
Seed / 3–18 months02

Will teams use it
and pay for it?

Run authorized shadow pilots, test repeated use and paid conversion, then establish secure delivery and measured unit economics.

Evidence gateNamed decision owners, recurring use, paid renewal and delivery costs that support the business.
