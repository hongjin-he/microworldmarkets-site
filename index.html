05 / Research

Toward a financial world model

Long term, MicroWorld will model major participants across markets—from institution types to institutions to desks. Learned and LLM-driven agents could scale the model to 10⁵–10⁶ participants, so decisions, policies and shocks can be tested before they happen.

The open-source MicroWorld repository is our research lineage, separate from the product engine.
