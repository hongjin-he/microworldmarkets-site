MICROWORLD BY ALPHAFLOW
A product by AlphaFlow. An interactive world model of financial markets.
Investor brief — October 2026

THESIS
Markets are networks of participants operating under constraints. MicroWorld is building a way to explore how those constraints transmit shocks into portfolio risk. Start with a precise, testable workflow; expand only when evidence supports it.

FIRST CUSTOMER AND PRODUCT
Initial hypothesis: an APAC professional investment team reviewing concentrated US equity/ETF exposure. Inputs: authorized holdings, a dated snapshot and a defined event. Outputs: scenario comparisons, rule traces, influential assumptions and an audit trail. Use: portfolio and risk-review discussions.

EVIDENCE TODAY
Working synthetic browser demo; earlier research code and prototype work; calibration/evaluation designs. No validated real-market predictive advantage, paid customers, committed pilots, production deployment, formal endorsement or committed investment. The venture is founder-reported as unincorporated and unfunded.

FIRST 90 DAYS AFTER INITIAL CAPITAL AND DATA ACCESS
Secure data rights. Freeze a held-out evaluation. Compare against historical simulation, EWMA and quantile regression with equal information and tuning budgets. Run ablations, quantify uncertainty, seek independent review. Conduct task-specific buyer interviews and qualify authorized shadow pilots.

BUSINESS MODEL
A scoped six-week paid pilot (US$5,000 price hypothesis), followed by annual team subscriptions. Platform licenses and usage-based API distribution follow repeatability. Annual pricing, willingness to pay, retention and delivery economics remain unvalidated.

FINANCING
US$5m financing ambition for an 18-month program. Planning allocation: US$1.8m core talent, 0.7m data and rights, 0.65m compute, 0.45m product/security, 0.4m pilots, 0.3m legal/operations, 0.7m contingency. Not vendor quotations or committed spending. Initial check size, valuation and terms are unset. Smaller first closes require a narrower funded plan.

LONG-TERM WORLD MODEL
Versioned observable states; calibrated constraints and transitions; adaptive participant populations; cross-market interactions; interventions with explicit uncertainty and out-of-domain warnings. Individual-level behavior is a longer-term research direction requiring appropriate data, consent and validation.
